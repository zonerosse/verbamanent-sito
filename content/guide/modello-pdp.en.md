The **PDP**, the personalised learning plan (*Piano Didattico Personalizzato*), is written by the school. But a good PDP almost always starts with a family that comes to the meeting knowing what to ask for. This template is for exactly that: **tick the tools and measures that are needed**, and an orderly A4 sheet writes itself alongside, ready to print, save as PDF or download in Word.

It also works for **university**, where there is no PDP but measures are requested from the university's SpLD service. It is free, no sign-up is needed and **what you write stays on your computer**: nothing is sent to anyone.

## How to use it, in 4 steps

1. **Choose "School" or "University"**: the items change (at university there is no copying from the board, for instance) and so do the signatures at the bottom.
2. **Write the name, class and diagnosis** as they appear in the certificate. The subjects where it matters most are optional, but they help the class council.
3. **Tick only what is really needed.** The first items are already ticked because they are the most common: untick those that do not apply. Under some items you will find the Verba Manent feature that does that job, if you are interested.
4. **Print it or save it as PDF** (with "Print or save as PDF" choose "Save as PDF" as the printer), or download it in **Word** to edit it further.

The template remembers what you wrote even if you close the page, but only on this device. To start from scratch, tap "Start again".

## What each item means

### Assistive tools

These are the tools that **replace or ease** what the disorder makes difficult.

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Text-to-speech** | Turns reading into listening: books, notes, test instructions |
| **Recording lessons** | Lets the student listen without having to write everything down, and listen again at home |
| **Maps and formula sheets** | Help retrieve key words, dates and formulas, also in tests |
| **Word processor with spell checker** | Replaces handwriting and flags spelling mistakes |
| **Speech-to-text dictation** | Writing by speaking: useful for homework, summaries, open answers |
| **Calculator, tables** | Compensate for mental arithmetic and memorising rules and times tables |
| **Digital textbooks and audiobooks** | The textbook in a format text-to-speech can read |
| **Highly legible font** | Larger, more spaced text on a tinted background: less effort to read |
| **Computer or tablet** | To use the tools above in class and in tests |

### Exemptions

They remove or adapt a task that is very hard because of the disorder and **does not improve learning**: reading aloud in front of the class, copying from the board, learning lists and dates by heart, writing in cursive, taking notes under fast dictation, written tests in foreign languages.

### Tests and assessment

They state **how** tests are done and **what** is assessed: extra time (often 30%) or fewer exercises, scheduled tests, instructions read aloud, oral instead of written, structured tests, accessible test sheets. And above all: **assess content, not form**, meaning the mark is not lowered for spelling or handwriting.

To find out more: [Assistive tools and exemptions: list and examples](/en/guides/assistive-tools-dyslexia/).

## At the meeting with the school

- **Bring the printed sheet and the diagnosis.** The sheet does not replace the PDP: it is your proposal, to be discussed.
- **Ask for the measures to be written subject by subject**, with the moments when they are used (lessons, homework, tests).
- **Ask how tests will be done** in practice: who reads the instructions, how the map is used, how much extra time.
- **Ask for a date to review it** during the year.
- **Keep a copy of the signed PDP.**

All the steps, from diagnosis to the state exam, are in the [guide for parents](/en/guides/dyslexia-guide-for-parents/). If you want the teachers to read it, there is also the [guide for teachers](/en/guides/dyslexia-guide-for-teachers/).

## At university

At university the sheet goes to the **disability and SpLD service** of the university, together with the diagnosis. The guidelines provide for **up to 30% extra time** in exams, assistive tools (recording, text-to-speech, computer) and the option of preferring oral exams. Each university has its own procedure: find out at the start of the course. All the details in [Dyslexia at university](/en/guides/dyslexia-at-university/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the sheet have legal value?**
No. It is a proposal from the family (or the student). The official document is the PDP written and signed by the school, or the university's decision.

**Can I edit it after downloading?**
Yes: download it in Word and change it as you like.

**Where does the data I type go?**
Nowhere. The template runs entirely in your browser: nothing is sent to Verba Manent or anyone else. It is saved only on this device, until you tap "Start again" or clear your browser data.

**Is it suitable for primary school too?**
Yes. In primary school the PDP is written by the team of teachers. Tick only the items suitable for the child's age.
